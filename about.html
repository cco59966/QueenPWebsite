About Us 👑

The story behind Queen P Smoothies.

Meet Patricia Pierre

Queen P Smoothies started in Patricia's kitchen in 2019, inspired by her passion for healthy food that actually tastes good. What began as homemade smoothies quickly grew through community support into a lifestyle brand focused on flavorful, feel-good meals for every body and every goal.

After launching a food trailer in Boynton Beach, Florida in 2022, Queen P Smoothies now calls Houston, Texas home. Serving smoothies, wraps, and salads made with heart, culture, and community in mind. 👑

Patricia believes that great-tasting food brings people together. Every smoothie she crafts is a reflection of her heritage, her heart, and her deep love for the people she serves. When you sip a Queen P smoothie, you're tasting a little piece of her story.
